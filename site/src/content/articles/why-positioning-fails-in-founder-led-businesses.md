Positioning fails in founder-led businesses for one reason: the founder will not choose who the business is for, because every customer who ever paid feels like proof that everyone could.

## The decision underneath

Pick the segment that will pay the most next year, and write the home page for them first. Everyone else still finds you; they just are not the first sentence.
