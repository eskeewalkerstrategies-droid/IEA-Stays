## Pillars (CONFIRM: three to five)

| Pillar | Audience | The question underneath | Our angle |
|---|---|---|---|
| Positioning for founder-led brands | Founders of trading businesses with 5 to 200 people | "Why is our marketing not converting?" | Positioning is a choice of who pays, not a tagline. |
| Growth that survives execution | Same, plus marketing heads | "Which channel first, and how do we keep it going?" | The channel you will still be doing in a year. |
| AI in the business, practically | Founders and directors curious about AI | "Where does AI actually change my business?" | Judgment stays human; delivery gets automated. Show our own system. |
| Pricing and proposals for services | Consultants, agencies, studios | "How do we price and sell scoped work?" | Scope, ownership and day-count timelines. |

## Audience notes

- Decision-maker is the founder or a director. They read on the phone, in the evening.
- They distrust generic advice. Every article needs one real example.
- Indian context: GST, TRAI, DPDP, Hinglish in conversation, English in documents.

## Questions buyers ask (from the CRM and forms, keep adding)

- "Our positioning is muddy. How do we fix it without a rebrand?"
- "We spent on ads and nothing happened. What now?"
- "Should we do LinkedIn or YouTube?"
- "How do we use AI without hiring a team?"
- "What should a strategy engagement cost?"

## We will not write about

- Tool reviews and "best 10 tools" lists.
- Hype pieces on AI with no business example.
- Anything that needs us to scrape or republish others' data.
- Politics, finance tips, personal productivity.
